GFL Lumina : HR Policy QnA Assistant

Powered by Qlik Answers

GFL Lumina is an AI-powered HR Policy Assistant developed to help employees quickly access, understand, and navigate organizational policies and procedures.

The assistant uses Qlik Answers to provide intelligent responses based on the uploaded HR policy knowledge base. Users can ask questions related to HR processes, employee guidelines, workplace policies, and organizational practices.

Lumina is designed to simplify policy discovery, reduce manual searching through lengthy documents, and improve accessibility of critical HR information across the organization.

Please verify the cited sources provided in responses for maximum accuracy and policy reference.

Recruitment Paid Holidays Career Progression Employee Induction Planning Across Locations Workplace Guidelines Employee Documentation Organizational Procedures

Additional policies and organizational documents may be integrated into the knowledge base over time to further expand assistant capabilities.

Users may ask policy-related questions in natural language. Some example queries are listed below:

I have worked at GFL for 6 years and earn ₹85,000/month. Am I eligible for a housing loan?
What compensation applies if an employee takes 40 days of medical leave after surgery?
What documents does a candidate need to complete onboarding at GFL?
What ratings qualify an employee for promotion from Level V to Level IV?
What factors should be considered before rehiring a former employee?
What state-specific holidays are observed in addition to the mandatory national holidays?
